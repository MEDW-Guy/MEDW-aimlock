MEDW-aimlock
The script is suitable for a lot of games, including those with NPCs and custom models.
Player ESP (ON/OFF) – ESP for players.
Bot ESP (ON/OFF) – ESP for NPCs.
Model ESP (ON/OFF) – ESP for custom player/bot models.
Aimlock (ON/OFF) – basic player aimlock targeting the torso.
Head Aim (P) (ON/OFF) – toggle that enables aiming at the player's head.
Bot Aim (ON/OFF) – basic NPC aimlock targeting the torso.
Head Aim (B) (ON/OFF) – toggle that enables aiming at the NPC's head.
Predict (ON/OFF) – toggle that enables the prediction function for aimlock.
Fill – Chams fill transparency.
Outline – Chams outline transparency.
Smooth – smoothness of the aimlock.
The lowest slider is for choosing the color for the Chams function.
While holding RMB (locking onto a target), you will see the distance to the target in studs (Dist stat).
